Executive Summary

Autonomous, Zero-GC Network Defense & Recursive Resolution Engine

KOODOXZ is an open-source, deterministic networking ecosystem engineered in pure Rust. It is built to replace legacy DNS resolvers and vulnerable network perimeters with a unified, defense-in-depth architecture. By executing kernel-level packet drops (eBPF/XDP), zero-round-trip encrypted transport (DoQ), and direct iterative root queries (RFC 1035), Koodoxz guarantees sub-millisecond predictability, immunity to garbage-collection pauses, and complete autonomy from centralized commercial resolvers.

01. Wire-Speed Perimeter Drops volumetric attacks and SYN floods directly at the network card driver layer via eBPF before allocating kernel memory.
02. Encrypted Zero-RTT Transit Relays endpoint DNS traffic across public WANs via DNS over QUIC (RFC 9250), bypassing ISP inspection and UDP amplification.
03. Autonomous Root Sovereignty Resolves hierarchically from the 13 Root Servers (A through M) with zero tracking, lock-free LRU caches, and zero garbage collection.
