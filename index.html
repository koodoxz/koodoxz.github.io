Executive Summary

Autonomous, Zero-GC Network Defense & Recursive Resolution Stack

MECOB is an open-source, deterministic networking and DNS defense ecosystem engineered in pure Rust (hosted under @koodoxz). It replaces legacy resolvers and exposed perimeter services with a unified, defense-in-depth architecture. By combining kernel-level packet filtering (eBPF/XDP), zero-round-trip encrypted transport (DoQ), and direct iterative root queries (RFC 1035), MECOB guarantees sub-millisecond predictability, zero garbage-collection pauses, and complete autonomy from centralized commercial middleboxes.

01. Kernel Sentinel — Kalas Drops volumetric attacks and SYN floods directly at the network card driver layer via eBPF/XDP before allocating kernel memory.
02. Encrypted Transport — Caraka + Gapura Relays endpoint DNS traffic across public WANs via DNS over QUIC (RFC 9250), bypassing ISP inspection and UDP amplification.
03. Autonomous Root DNS — Runut Resolves hierarchically from the 13 Root Servers (A through M) with zero tracking, lock-free LRU caches, and zero garbage collection.
04. Threat Intelligence — Tameng Persistent behavioral L7 WAF analyzing anomalous query entropy, dynamically pushing IP blocks to Kalas and domain filters to Runut.
